Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Airy is a music player for your own audio files. It does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • Airy has no account and no sign-in.
  • Airy does not track you and contains no analytics, advertising or third-party code.
  • Your music files stay on your Mac. Airy only reads the files and folders you choose, to play them and show their tags and covers. Its playlist and settings are saved only on your Mac.
  • Purchases are made through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; Airy only receives a confirmation that the purchase was made.
