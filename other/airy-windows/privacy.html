Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Airy is a music player for your own audio files. It does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • Airy has no account and no sign-in.
  • Airy does not track you and contains no analytics, advertising or third-party code.
  • Your music files stay on your PC. Airy only reads the files and folders you choose, to play them and show their tags and covers. Its playlist and settings are saved only on your PC.
  • Airy connects to the internet only to talk to the Microsoft Store, for the purchase and for updates.
  • Purchases are made through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft processes the payment; Airy only receives a confirmation that the purchase was made.
