Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Grepy is a search app for your own files. It does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • Grepy has no account and no sign-in.
  • Grepy does not track you and contains no analytics, advertising or third-party code.
  • Grepy reads only the folders, files and archives you choose, and never changes them. To open them again later, it keeps a macOS bookmark to each one, saved only on your Mac.
  • Archives are unpacked into Grepy's own temporary folder on your Mac. They are deleted when you press Clear and when you quit.
  • Grepy makes no network connections. Nothing you search for or find leaves your Mac.
