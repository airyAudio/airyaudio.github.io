Airy Support

Airy is a native, lossless audio player for macOS 26 and later.

Adding Music

Click + (⌘O) or drag files and folders into the playlist. FLAC, ALAC, AIFF, WAV, CAF, MP3, AAC (M4A), AC-3, E-AC-3, CUE sheets and M3U/PLS/XSPF/WPL playlists are supported.

Why Only These Formats?

Airy plays everything macOS itself can play, with Apple's own audio engine and no third-party decoders. That keeps it light, fast and as reliable as macOS, with every song in its original quality. Files in other formats (OGG, Opus, WMA, APE, WavPack, DSD…) are still added to the playlist, greyed out, and skipped when playing.
