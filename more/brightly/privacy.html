Brightly for Windows

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Brightly is a brightness app for your displays. It does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • Brightly has no account and no sign-in.
  • Brightly does not track you and contains no analytics, advertising or third-party code.
  • Brightly talks only to your displays, to read and set their brightness. It never saves the brightness. The checkboxes you choose and your shortcut choice are saved only on your PC.
  • The tray icon reacts to the mouse wheel and to your keyboard's brightness keys only to change the brightness. No keystrokes, clicks or movements are recorded.
  • Brightly connects to the internet only to talk to the Microsoft Store, for the free trial, the purchase and updates. Microsoft processes the payment; Brightly only receives the licence state.
