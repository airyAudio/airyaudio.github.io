Support

How can we help?

Airy for Windows is a native, lossless audio player for Windows 10 and 11.

Adding music

Click + and choose Add Files… (Ctrl+O) or Add Folder… (Ctrl+Shift+O), or drag files and folders from Explorer into the playlist. FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, MP3, AAC (M4A), WMA, AC-3, E-AC-3, CUE sheets and M3U/PLS/XSPF/WPL playlists are supported.

Why only these formats?

Airy plays everything Windows itself can play, with Microsoft's own Media Foundation and no third-party decoders. That keeps it light, fast and as reliable as Windows, with every song in its original quality. Files in other formats (APE, WavPack, DSD, Musepack, TTA, TAK, MKA...) are still added to the playlist, greyed out, and skipped when playing.

Frequently asked questions

Which PCs does Airy run on?

Windows 10 version 2004 or later, or Windows 11, on x64 or ARM64 PCs.

Does Airy stream music or need an account?

No. Airy plays the music files on your PC and on drives you connect. There is no streaming service, no account and no sign-in.

What does Airy Upgrade include?

A 10-band equalizer, output device choice, Play Next and queue, Dynamic and Solid styles, Light/Dark and your own accent color, Title - Artist or Artist - Title, song info (also for several songs), playlist export, a resizable window with compact player sizes, hiding the cover, the volume bar or the window controls, your queue in the search field, Cover Mode (Keep Cover Mode on Top in the Window menu), pinned album names, Pin Artist, Collapse Albums, and every song action on right-click.

How do I restore my purchase?

Sign in to the Microsoft Store with the same Microsoft account, then open the ☰ menu and choose More > Restore Purchase.

Can I use Airy Upgrade on my other PCs?

Yes. It works on all your PCs with the same Microsoft account.

Why does Airy keep playing after I close the window?

Like a music app should: it stays in the notification area, next to the clock. Click its icon to bring the window back, or right-click it to play, pause, skip or quit.

Why is a song greyed out?

Either its format is one Windows has no decoder for (such as APE, WavPack or DSD, or OGG and Opus without Microsoft's Web Media Extensions), or its file isn't available right now, for example on a drive that isn't connected. Connect the drive and the song plays again.

Why won't MP3, AAC or FLAC play on my PC?

Windows "N" editions (sold mostly in Europe) come without Windows' media playback. Install Microsoft's free Media Feature Pack (Settings > Apps > Optional features), and MP3, AAC, FLAC and the rest play in Airy.

Where does Airy save my playlist and settings?

Only on your PC, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Airy. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
