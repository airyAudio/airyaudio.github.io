How to use mouse back and forward buttons on a Mac

Updated November 2026

Short answer: macOS doesn't turn a mouse's side buttons into Back and Forward, so in Safari and the Finder they do nothing. Chrome, Firefox and other apps that handle the buttons themselves work already. For the rest, use an app such as Conventional Mouse, which turns the buttons into each app's own Back and Forward shortcut.

Why the buttons do nothing

A mouse sends its side buttons as buttons 4 and 5. Windows turns them into Back and Forward everywhere. macOS passes them on to the app, and most Apple apps don't listen for them.

Make them work with Conventional Mouse

  1. Download Conventional Mouse+ and move it to the Applications folder. Conventional Mouse is coming to the Mac App Store.
  2. Open it and allow Accessibility in System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  3. Make sure Buttons is on in its menu bar menu.

The side buttons now go Back and Forward in Safari, the Finder, the App Store, System Settings, Help, Music, TV and Xcode. Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Arc, VS Code and JetBrains IDEs get the clicks unchanged, as before.

Frequently asked questions

Do the buttons work in every app?

Only in apps that have Back and Forward. In other apps the clicks pass through unchanged, so a button never shifts text in an editor.

Does it need a special mouse?

No. Any mouse with side buttons works, wired or wireless.
