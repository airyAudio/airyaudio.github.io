Why Airy

Your music, the way it was recorded.

Big artwork and a quiet, beautiful window that feels like it belongs to macOS.

Lossless and hi-res

FLAC, ALAC, AIFF, WAV and CAF in their original resolution. Airy shows each file's real format, e.g. FLAC 24/96.

Gapless playback

Live albums, DJ mixes and classical works play without a break between tracks.

Native and light

Built only with Apple's frameworks. Small, fast to start, easy on the battery and as stable as macOS.

Liquid Glass

Apple's Liquid Glass design from macOS 26, with glass sliders and buttons, in Light and Dark.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no ads. Your music and your playlist stay on your Mac.

Album-first playlist

Songs grouped by album, with covers. Drag and drop to add and reorder, instant search, undo and redo.

Styles and colors

Light, Dark and your own color.

Airy follows macOS Light and Dark, or gets its own. Pick an accent color, or let the Dynamic color follow each album cover.

Frosted, Dynamic and Solid styles change how much of your desktop shines through.

Playlist

Your music, your way.

Pin Artist shows only one artist's albums, with their covers on top. Collapse Albums turns a long playlist into a tidy list of album names, and the one you pick opens.

Make the window as small as a compact player, or hide the cover, the volume bar or the window controls.

Equalizer, Output and Info

Tune every band.

A 10-band equalizer with 13 presets and your own, plus Level correction against distortion.

Send Airy to speakers, headphones, a USB DAC or AirPlay right from the volume bar, without changing the sound output of the rest of your Mac.

Song Info shows every tag and file detail: format, sample rate, bit depth, bitrate, size and location. Also for several songs at once.

Cover Mode

Just the artwork.

Pinch out on the cover, or press it firmly, and the album art fills the window. The controls appear as you move the mouse, and your playlist is one click away.

Play Next and Add to Queue, with your queue right in the search field.

Formats

Everything your Mac can play.

Airy uses Apple's own audio engine, with no third-party decoders. It gets better with every macOS update.

FLAC ALAC AIFF WAV CAF MP3 AAC (M4A) AC-3 E-AC-3 CUE sheets M3U / M3U8 PLS XSPF WPL

Files in other formats (OGG, Opus, WMA, APE, WavPack, DSD...) are still added to the playlist, greyed out, so nothing goes missing. They are skipped when playing.

Support

How can we help?

Answers to common questions, or write to us.

Adding music

Click + (⌘O) or drag files and folders into the playlist. FLAC, ALAC, AIFF, WAV, CAF, MP3, AAC (M4A), AC-3, E-AC-3, CUE sheets and M3U/PLS/XSPF/WPL playlists are supported.

Drag songs to reorder them. Changes can be undone, and Airy remembers your playlist the next time you open it.

Why only these formats?

Airy plays everything macOS itself can play, with Apple's own audio engine and no third-party decoders. That keeps it light, fast and as reliable as macOS, with every song in its original quality. Files in other formats (OGG, Opus, WMA, APE, WavPack, DSD...) are still added to the playlist, greyed out, and skipped when playing.

Frequently asked questions

Which Macs does Airy run on?

Any Mac with macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later, with Apple silicon or Intel.

Does Airy stream music or need an account?

No. Airy plays the music files on your Mac and on drives you connect. There is no streaming service, no account and no sign-in.

What does Airy Upgrade include?

The 10-band equalizer, output device choice, Play Next and the queue, Cover Mode, Dynamic and Solid styles with your own accent color, Pin Artist, Collapse Albums, song info and more. One purchase, yours forever, with Family Sharing.

To open Cover Mode, pinch out on the cover or press it firmly. Pinch in or press Esc to go back.

See Free and Upgrade side by side

How do I restore my purchase?

Bought on another Mac or reinstalled? Choose More > Restore Purchase.

Can my family use Airy Upgrade?

Yes. Airy Upgrade supports Family Sharing, so people in your family group get it without buying it again.

Why is a song greyed out?

Either its format is one macOS can't play (such as OGG, Opus, WMA, APE, WavPack or DSD), or its file isn't available right now, for example on a drive that isn't connected. Connect the drive and the song plays again.

Where does Airy save my playlist and settings?

Only on your Mac, inside Airy's own app folder. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
