Why Mixy

Mix like in the club.

No account, no streaming, no tracking. Just your music files and your hands. Offline.

Two decks, club style

Play, CUE, SEARCH, the jog wheel, the pitch fader and the time, like the classic club CD players. Bend while playing, search while paused.

The whole mixer

Trim, a 3-band EQ or isolator with kills, a filter, channel faders, the crossfader with three curves and a master with a limiter.

Headphone cue

Hear the next track before the crowd does, on its own output: headphones, a second sound card or your controller's jack.

DJ controllers

Plug in a MIDI controller and play. Mappings for about 40 popular controllers are built in, and you can map any other one in a minute.

Record your mix

Hold REC and Mixy records what your speakers play, to a WAV file. Set History keeps the tracks you played, to copy or export.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no ads. Built only with Apple's own frameworks. Your music stays on your Mac, and Mixy never writes to your files.

Waveforms, BPM and Sync

See the beat.

Big waveforms with the beat grid, in color, peaks or loudness, and an overview of the whole track. BPM and musical key found by Mixy, or read from your files' tags. Auto Gain makes tracks play about equally loud.

SYNC matches the other deck's tempo, and B (Beats) keeps the beats together too. 70 and 140 BPM count as one groove. Keylock keeps the key while you change the tempo.

Your music

The playlist, right on the deck.

Each deck has its own playlist, with search, sort and a queue. BROWSE turns the deck's screen from the waveform to the list, or to your Mac's folders.

Cue points, hot cues, loops and beat grids stay with each track. Hot cues, loops and beat grids that other DJ software saved in your files are read too.

Button layouts

Your layout, your jog wheel.

Eight button layouts: rows, CD player, controller, compact, club player, around the jog wheel, pads and classic. Drag the line between the screen and the jog wheel, and the jog wheel, its buttons and the pitch fader grow.

Hot cues, loops (IN, OUT, EXIT, 4-beat), Quantize, Slip, Reverse and Brake, all one click away. On a controller you get 8 hot cues, beat jump and beat loop pads.

Hands on

Your hands on the music.

With VINYL on, two fingers on the jog wheel scratch, or circle them to turn it like a record. In CD mode the top bends the tempo while playing. Swipe the waveform, flick it and it glides on.

Hold a key and scroll to turn a knob, from anywhere on the screen.

Keyboard shortcuts for play, cue, hot cues, sync, loops and more, and the media keys play and pause the decks.

Formats

Your tracks, as they are.

Mixy plays your own music files with Apple's own audio frameworks, and never changes them.

ALAC FLAC WAV AIFF CAF MP3 AAC (M4A) M3U playlists

Playlists can be exported and imported, also as M3U. A whole track is kept in memory while it plays, for instant cues and scratching, so a track can be up to 4 hours long.

Support

How can we help?

Mixy is a native DJ app for macOS: two decks and a mixer for your own music.

Adding music

Drag tracks or folders from the Finder into a deck's playlist, or press BROWSE and open your folders right in the deck. Each deck has its own playlist. To load a track, drag it onto its deck, double-click it, or select it and press Return.

Using a DJ controller

Plug in a MIDI controller. If its mapping is built in, it works right away. For any other controller, open Settings > Controllers and choose Record Own Mapping to go step by step, or Map by Clicking to click a control on screen and then move it on the controller.

Frequently asked questions

Which Macs does Mixy run on?

macOS 26 or later, on Macs with Apple silicon or Intel.

Does Mixy stream music or need an account?

No. Mixy plays the music files on your Mac and on drives you connect. There is no streaming service, no account and no sign-in.

How does the free trial work?

Open Mixy > Upgrade Mixy… and choose to start the free trial. It is a free purchase through the App Store, so it starts only when you choose and runs for 14 days. After that, keep everything with Mixy Upgrade, or go on with Mixy, free.

What does Mixy Upgrade include?

Big waveforms with the beat grid and the overview, BPM and musical key, Sync and Beats, Keylock and the pitch range, hot cues, loops and Quantize, Slip, Reverse and Brake, VINYL scratching, Auto Gain, preview in the headphones and the eight button layouts.

How do I restore my purchase?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account, then open Mixy > Upgrade Mixy… and choose Restore Purchases.

Can my family use Mixy Upgrade?

Yes. Mixy Upgrade works on all your Macs with the same Apple Account, and with Family Sharing your family can use it too.

Which DJ controllers have a built-in mapping?

About 40 popular controllers, made from the makers' own MIDI lists. The full list is in Mixy's help, under DJ controller. Any other MIDI controller can be mapped by you, and mappings can be exported and imported. Controllers that work only over HID, not MIDI, are not supported; many of them have a MIDI mode.

Soft takeover keeps a knob or fader from jumping when the controller and the screen differ, and the channel level LEDs light where the controller has them.

How do I record my mix?

Hold REC in the middle of the mixer for 1.5 seconds. Mixy records exactly what your speakers get, as a 24-bit WAV file in your Downloads folder. Hold REC again to stop, and the Finder shows the file.

Can I see which tracks I played?

Yes. Choose Window > Set History (⌘Y). A track is added once it has played 30 seconds in the speakers. Copy or export each set as a text list.

How do I hear the next track in my headphones?

Choose the headphone output in Settings > Audio Output: a second sound card, or the headphone jack of your controller's sound card. Then press the headphones button on a channel. CUE/MST blends the cue with the master, and LEVEL sets the volume. With Mixy Upgrade you can also right-click a track in the playlist and choose Preview in Headphones.

Does Mixy change my music files?

No. Mixy only reads your files. Cue points, hot cues, loops and beat grids are saved in Mixy's own playlists, not in your files.

Where is the help?

Click ⓘ on the mixer or choose Help > Mixy Help (⌘?). The help shows every part of Mixy, with every keyboard shortcut, in 10 languages: English, Deutsch, Español, Français, हिन्दी, Italiano, 日本語, Polski, Українська and 中文.

Where does Mixy save my playlists and settings?

Only on your Mac, in Mixy's own folder. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
