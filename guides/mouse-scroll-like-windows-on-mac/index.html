How to make a mouse scroll like Windows on a Mac

Updated November 2026

Short answer: macOS speeds the mouse wheel up as you turn it, and its Natural Scrolling setting is shared by the trackpad and the mouse. To scroll like Windows, a fixed 3 lines a notch in the classic direction, while the trackpad stays natural, use a small menu bar app such as Conventional Mouse.

Why a mouse feels different on a Mac

Windows scrolls a fixed number of lines for each notch of the wheel. macOS adds acceleration: slow notches move a little, fast ones a lot. It is made for the trackpad and feels jumpy on a notched wheel.

The direction is the other half. In System Settings, Natural Scrolling for the mouse and for the trackpad is one switch: turn it off for the mouse, and the trackpad scrolls the old way too.

Scroll like Windows with Conventional Mouse

  1. Download Conventional Mouse+ and move it to the Applications folder. Conventional Mouse is coming to the Mac App Store.
  2. Open it and allow Accessibility in System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  3. Make sure Scroll Wheel is on in its menu bar menu.

Each notch now scrolls a fixed 3 lines in the classic direction. Your trackpad and a Magic Mouse keep scrolling as before. Conventional Mouse+ can also give the pointer Windows' speed and acceleration.
